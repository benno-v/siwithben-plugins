# Risk Register: Sustainable Fashion Launch Campaign

**Context:** Marketing campaign, sustainable fashion. Gen Z launch campaign, USD 300,000 over six months, channels: Instagram, TikTok, influencer partnerships, email and SEO.
**Scoring:** H = 3, M = 2, L = 1. Score = Probability x Impact (1 to 9). 6 to 9 High, 3 to 4 Medium, 1 to 2 Low.

## Top 3 to discuss with the sponsor
1. R01: greenwashing exposure is the main reputational threat for a sustainable fashion brand targeting a sceptical Gen Z audience.
2. R03: there is no point driving demand without stock, so the sponsor needs to back a stock-readiness gate before launch.
3. R05: without agreed attribution, the sponsor will not be able to judge return on the USD 300,000 spend.
